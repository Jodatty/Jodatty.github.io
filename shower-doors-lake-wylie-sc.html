Questions

Common Questions

Do you serve Lake Wylie, South Carolina?

Yes. We install in Lake Wylie and the greater Charlotte metro area. We quote Lake Wylie projects the same way as any other: send photos, get a written price, and confirm final measurements on site before any glass is ordered.

What should I send for a Lake Wylie estimate?

Send a straight-on photo of the shower opening, side views, rough dimensions and any drawings or inspiration photos. You can upload them from your phone, even before the tile is set. We reply with a written price before you commit to anything.

Can I plan the glass before the tile is installed?

Yes, and it is the best time to do it. Door swing, floor slope and details such as a water dam are easiest to settle before the floor is finished. If you are planning a curbless shower, read Plan for the Door, Not Just the Drain.

How much does a custom frameless shower door cost?

We quote most projects directly from photos you send us, even rough framing or elevation drawings, before tile is installed. Final measure happens after we agree on a price in principle, and it rarely changes the number. See our full process.

How long does a shower door installation take?

Once glass is fabricated to your final measurements, installation is typically completed in a single visit. Glass is ordered after measurement and arrives within five to ten business days depending on the configuration.
